## Confirmed facts
- Church 1925/1945-53 style, cross-shaped compact plan, slanted walls; front gable: windowless pale-ochre/cream plaster, framed by vertical white pilasters (5 fields), wide-eaved shallow low-pitch roof; open porch with 5 openings, wide stair, perforated brick-screen panels left/right of porch; paved natural-stone forecourt (cobbles/stone slabs), U-shaped ensemble with Gemeindehaus (ochre, hipped tile roof) and two parsonages. Porch inscription John 10:16.
- Freestanding concrete bell tower (pale concrete, open bell stage with 5 bells, blue/iridescent clock faces by Olga Titus, weathervane cross).
- Rathaus conversion 2023-2027: interior only (pulpit removed, curtain at organ, ceiling rings, foyer, 50-seat gallery). No exterior changes/banners documented. Signage "Rathaus Hard" not documented in sources.
- Sources: https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bullingerkirche_(Z%C3%BCrich-Hard) ; https://www.kantonsrat.zh.ch/besuch/provisorium/ ; https://www.zh.ch/de/planen-bauen/hochbau/bauprojekte/bauprojekte-hochbauamt/weitere-bauten/rathaus-zuerich-p-rovisorium-kirche-hard.html ; Commons category Bullingerkirche (Zürich) (CC BY-SA files already in v3/bullingerkirche).
