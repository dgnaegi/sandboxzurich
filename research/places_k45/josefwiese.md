# Josefwiese (Kreis 5)
Lage: WGS84 47.38585,8.5231; lokal x=486 y=201 (m, Radius der Analyse 120 m). Vorschau-Kontaktbogen: preview/ref_k45_josefwiese.jpg, Fotos: data/raw/photos/places_k45/josefwiese/NN.jpg
Hinweis: Tramlinien/Namen mit '?' unverifiziert; Hoehen aus OSM/Web; Farben aus Sicht (Naeherung).

## Charakter
Large public park (~1.5 ha) between Viaduktstrasse, Josefstrasse and Roentgenplatz; big open lawn framed by trees, former brickworks/Quartierpark; mix of sport, sunbathing, boules, playground.

## Landmarks (Sichtbefunde)
Oval/rectangular open lawn (photo 1,3) with Viadukt arcade (S edge, stone arches with glass shopfronts, photo 2). Backdrop: tall white round concrete chimney of Energiezentrale/KVA Josefstrasse (~90 m, photos 1,3,6) and the teal Prime Tower in the distance (photos 1,8). Granite fountain with column + bird (photo 2): wide shallow bowl, low round stone rim wall, cobbles around. Kiosk Josefwiese (photo 5,6,7): small brown wooden hut, hipped dark roof, glass front; restaurant terrace with red plastic chairs and parasols on grey gravel (photo 7). Planschbecken (paddling pool) at ~(480,200). Playground (photo 4): raised wooden tower on steel posts with chain-link climbing wall and slide, pebbled gravel ground, pines behind.

## Fassadenfarben/-stile
Viadukt arcade stone grey-beige. Residential edge 5-6 storey 1920s blocks (Roentgenhof, 1928) light plaster.

## Bodenbelaege
Lawn, grey fine gravel (Kies) paths and plazas, boules court (Petanque Club building, brick, skillion roof, at ~518,253), wood-chip/pebble in playground.

## Stadtmobiliar
Public toilets (2 nodes), many benches, drinking fountain, long row of tall columnar poplars (photo 1, 8) along E edge, pines (Foehre) clusters (photo 5,7), autumn yellow lime/maple (photo 8).

## OeV
Tram 4/13 Limmatplatz (~350 m E), bus 32 / tram 17 Escher-Wyss-Platz; bike rental node.

## Gruen
Predominantly lawn + conifers + poplars; very open view to Prime Tower.

## Laeden/Bars (Fakten)
Kiosk Josefwiese (fast food/kiosk), Jenseits Wohnzimmer cafe, Restaurant Viadukt (2 nodes) at arch row.

## Atmosphaere
Sunny afternoons: crowds on lawn, boules players in autumn (photo 8), tall chimney always in view.
