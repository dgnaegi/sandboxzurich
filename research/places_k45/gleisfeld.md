# Bahnareal / Gleisfeld (Kreis 4)
Lage: WGS84 47.3802,8.525; lokal x=638 y=-425 (m, Radius der Analyse 250 m). Vorschau-Kontaktbogen: preview/ref_k45_gleisfeld.jpg, Fotos: data/raw/photos/places_k45/gleisfeld/NN.jpg
Hinweis: Tramlinien/Namen mit '?' unverifiziert; Hoehen aus OSM/Web; Farben aus Sicht (Naeherung).

## Charakter
Bahnareal north of Langstrasse/Hohlstrasse: SBB track field with long rails, signals, Langstrassenunterfuehrung.

## Landmarks (Sichtbefunde)
Langstrassenunterfuehrung (photos 1-6): concrete ceiling grey, long fluorescent lights, walls painted pale blue/green with leaf murals and heavy graffiti (pink tag), ground painted blue-green with orange bike lane markings and dashed yellow separator, walls with lighter plaster, small signs 'Swiss Life Arena'. Track fields (photos 7-9): dense parallel track fans, red SBB locos (Re 4/4, 460 type) and double-deck IC2000 stock, steel gantries with catenary, tall lamp masts, Prime Tower (photo 9) and Maag chimney on horizon. Stations: old Bahnhof (photo 10: blue-grey shed with graffiti, historic sandstone building with arched windows), concrete ramp (photo 11).

## Fassadenfarben/-stile
Dark brown/black Bahn sheds, blue-grey metal, sandstone old station (#c5b490).

## Bodenbelaege
Ballast, concrete, asphalt, blue-green bike paint.

## Stadtmobiliar
Gantries, signal posts, high lamp masts, fences.

## OeV
S-Bahn via Hardbruecke, HB.

## Gruen
Hardly; tracks vegetation.

## Laeden/Bars (Fakten)
Hotel Bar, Maria Bar, OMA MAO, La Strega, Eichhoernli, Hochparterre Buecher, Gaucho.

## Atmosphaere
Industrial rail view.
