# Kalkbreite (Genossenschaft + Tramdepot) (Kreis 4)
Lage: WGS84 47.3733,8.5209; lokal x=339 y=-1196 (m, Radius der Analyse 130 m). Vorschau-Kontaktbogen: preview/ref_k45_kalkbreite.jpg, Fotos: data/raw/photos/places_k45/kalkbreite/NN.jpg
Hinweis: Tramlinien/Namen mit '?' unverifiziert; Hoehen aus OSM/Web; Farben aus Sicht (Naeherung).

## Charakter
Genossenschaft Kalkbreite (Müller Sigrist, 2014): 7-storey housing/commercial block above active tram depot; courtyard 'Wohnzimmer' on top of tram hall. Elisabethen/Kalkbreite.

## Landmarks (Sichtbefunde)
Tram depot (photos 1,4,5,6,8): old hall with tall grey-brown industrial hall, large stepped metal door; interior with curved tracks, concrete columns, white/grey. Trams: old Tram 2000 blue/white (photos 2,3, line 14). New building (photos 10,11): beige/cream plaster (#e6d9b5) with fine horizontal window grid, balconies of dark steel, setbacks/cascading roof terraces; hybrid timber-element facades per web; courtyard gravel garden, round red/white tables and chairs, red parasols, wooden benches. Old long glass hall with brick base (photos 7,9: continuous clerestory glass strip, church spire of Elisabethenkirche behind, platform of Wiedikon station).

## Fassadenfarben/-stile
Cream/beige render, black-grey window frames, brown stone retaining wall.

## Bodenbelaege
Gravel in courtyard, concrete, asphalt, tram tracks.

## Stadtmobiliar
Round bistro tables (red), wooden bench steps, trellis/hanging.

## OeV
Tram 2/3/9 Kalkbreite/Bhf Wiedikon, tram 14 depot, Wiedikon.

## Gruen
Rooftop gardens, 42 trees.

## Laeden/Bars (Fakten)
Alternative Bank Schweiz, Simply Soup, Rémy, di Lenardo; cafe Kalkbreite.

## Atmosphaere
Urban cooperative, daytime student/lounge.
