# Schiffbau (Kreis 5)
Lage: WGS84 47.3889,8.5189; lokal x=164 y=536 (m, Radius der Analyse 130 m). Vorschau-Kontaktbogen: preview/ref_k45_schiffbau.jpg, Fotos: data/raw/photos/places_k45/schiffbau/NN.jpg
Hinweis: Tramlinien/Namen mit '?' unverifiziert; Hoehen aus OSM/Web; Farben aus Sicht (Naeherung).

## Charakter
Former Escher-Wyss ship-building hall (1892-94), now Schauspielhaus Zuerich venues (Halle, Box, Matchbox), restaurant LaSalle, Moods jazz club.

## Landmarks (Sichtbefunde)
Brick hall (photos 4,6,7,11): yellow-brown (#c9a56a) brick wall with red-orange brick arch bands (#b5442a) round arched large windows with white/black steel glazing bars, grey stone plinth, gable top with stepped parapet, corner pilasters, large central arch with 'SCHIFFBAU' lettering in black on glass; ~13 m high, steel doors with graffiti. Foreground dark rust sculpture (propeller/turbine, photo 6) with bollard. Interior (5,8,9,10): steel-truss roof, patinated concrete pillars, wooden parquet floor, brown leather sofas, long dark bar. LaSalle restaurant glass box with white tablecloths (3). Neighbour complex: new 6-8 storey housing blocks (photo 2 aerial).

## Fassadenfarben/-stile
Yellow brick + red brick bands (heritage Escher-Wyss 1891).

## Bodenbelaege
Asphalt/concrete plates, sett, light gravel plaza.

## Stadtmobiliar
Rust steel sculptures, black bollards, glass panel signs.

## OeV
Tram 4/13? Tram Schiffbau (platform, tram 4 no; line 17 at Schiffbau: platform), bus 32, 72.

## Gruen
Few trees (small).

## Laeden/Bars (Fakten)
Schiffbau theatre, LaSalle, Spoon, Capucho, Moods, Labor5, Exil, Aubrey, Ahoi!.

## Atmosphaere
Industrial-culture.
