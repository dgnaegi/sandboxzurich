# Turbinenplatz (Kreis 5)
Lage: WGS84 47.3898,8.5167; lokal x=-4 y=634 (m, Radius der Analyse 120 m). Vorschau-Kontaktbogen: preview/ref_k45_turbinenplatz.jpg, Fotos: data/raw/photos/places_k45/turbinenplatz/NN.jpg
Hinweis: Tramlinien/Namen mit '?' unverifiziert; Hoehen aus OSM/Web; Farben aus Sicht (Naeherung).

## Charakter
Large paved square (ca 120 x 90 m) of 2000s Zuerich-West redevelopment between Technopark, Puls 5, hotels (Novotel), Giessereihalle remnants.

## Landmarks (Sichtbefunde)
Large-format light-grey concrete slabs with thin joint lines (photos 1,2), birch trees (Birken) planted in groups in gravel pits (photos 2,6), wooden sleeper benches (photo 4,6,8: dark weathered timber blocks), led-lit lines at night; rust-coloured Pelton/Francis turbine runner on tall concrete pylon (photo 3) as monument; Puls 5 building (photo 5,9): long 6-storey with green patinated metal-panel/ corrugated shingle facade (grey-green), long continuous balconies, white 'PULS 5' lettering; MAN blue corrugated hall (photo 5,7). Neon text installation at night (10,11).

## Fassadenfarben/-stile
Grey-green (#8aa39a) metal cladding, light concrete/grey, blue corrugated hall (#2a4f8a).

## Bodenbelaege
Grey concrete slabs, gravel (Splitt) planter fields, asphalt streets with yellow parking markings.

## Stadtmobiliar
Dark timber blocks, concrete round bollards, tall slim lamp masts with dual heads, neon art.

## OeV
Tram 4 Escher-Wyss/ Schiffbau; bus 32.

## Gruen
Birch groups.

## Laeden/Bars (Fakten)
Angkor, Noodle House, Hokkaido, Marmar, Novotel cafe, Aubrey.

## Atmosphaere
Open, windy, event-ish.
