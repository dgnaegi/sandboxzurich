# Hardplatz (Kreis 4)
Lage: WGS84 47.3825,8.5147; lokal x=-144 y=-180 (m, Radius der Analyse 110 m). Vorschau-Kontaktbogen: preview/ref_k45_hardplatz.jpg, Fotos: data/raw/photos/places_k45/hardplatz/NN.jpg
Hinweis: Tramlinien/Namen mit '?' unverifiziert; Hoehen aus OSM/Web; Farben aus Sicht (Naeherung).

## Charakter
Large tram stop plaza in Aussersihl; cobblestone; Wohnsiedlung Sonnenheim around.

## Landmarks (Sichtbefunde)
Paved plaza with 'Kleinpflaster/Pflästerung' in blue-grey (photos 2,3) and tram tracks; tram canopy long, flat; Hardplatz sign 'Hardplatz 8 Bahnhof Selnau Klusplatz' green/blue; brown bronze bent-pipe drinking fountain in gravel with trees (photo 5); red-orange 5-6 storey brick-plaster blocks 1930s with flat/hip roofs (photos 2,4); 'Hardschloss' white rusticated corner building (photo 5). Historic yellow Limmattal-Strassenbahn tram (photos 8,9: yellow/red, wooden with clerestory) at Oldtimer events.

## Fassadenfarben/-stile
Brick orange (#c0704a) plaster, white rustica, yellow.

## Bodenbelaege
Cobble inlays, asphalt, gravel under trees.

## Stadtmobiliar
Bent bronze fountain, benches 37, clocks 3, shelters 5.

## OeV
Tram 8 (Hardplatz), bus 72?, Hardbruecke 400 m.

## Gruen
Plane trees in gravel.

## Laeden/Bars (Fakten)
Caffe Spettacolo, avec 24/7, D'Ouro, Giro d'Italia ice cream.

## Atmosphaere
Wide, open, calm.
