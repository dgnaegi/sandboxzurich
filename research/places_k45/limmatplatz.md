# Limmatplatz (Kreis 5)
Lage: WGS84 47.3847,8.5316; lokal x=1129 y=82 (m, Radius der Analyse 130 m). Vorschau-Kontaktbogen: preview/ref_k45_limmatplatz.jpg, Fotos: data/raw/photos/places_k45/limmatplatz/NN.jpg
Hinweis: Tramlinien/Namen mit '?' unverifiziert; Hoehen aus OSM/Web; Farben aus Sicht (Naeherung).

## Charakter
Busy tram/bus intersection (Langstrasse/Limmatstrasse/Josefstrasse), pedestrian-heavy, 1960s-70s tram shelters, Kreis 5 entrance.

## Landmarks (Sichtbefunde)
Canopy shelters (photos 5,7,8,9): thin white/light-grey concrete mushroom roof (curved overhang) over 3 glazed cylindrical towers with translucent white glass vertical strips lit amber from within at night (kiosk/WC/infopoint, ~3 m diameter). Rest of space: cobblestone/yellow zebra stripe markings. Bronze/dark patinated bowl fountain on concrete foot (photo 1). Bronze bent-pipe public drinking fountain on stone slab (photo 2). Tall office slab (photo 3: grey/white curtain-wall with red signage, ~15 storeys, Migros HQ? unconfirmed) behind.

## Fassadenfarben/-stile
Neighbour houses 1890s-1900s 4-6 storey plastered, ochre, grey, with shutters; Limmatplatz 3a-e small 1-storey service kiosks (flat roofs); Kornhausbruecke apartments 6 storeys.

## Bodenbelaege
Asphalt, yellow thermoplastic zebra crossings (broad bars), tram track bed in asphalt, large-format concrete/cobble island paving.

## Stadtmobiliar
Tall slender grey masts (VBZ), overhead tram wires (bright), bollards, glass advertising vitrines, round kiosks.

## OeV
Tram 4, 13, 17 (Cobra blue-white), bus 31/32/46.

## Gruen
Plane trees along edges (photo 4: large, light canopy).

## Laeden/Bars (Fakten)
Dense: kiosks, snack bars, optical, ATM; Langstrasse starts. See OSM.

## Atmosphaere
Evening: amber glow of cylinders, wet asphalt, yellow lines.
