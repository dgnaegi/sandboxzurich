# Hohlstrasse (Kreis 4) (Kreis 4)
Lage: WGS84 47.3776,8.5215; lokal x=377 y=-718 (m, Radius der Analyse 250 m). Vorschau-Kontaktbogen: preview/ref_k45_hohlstrasse.jpg, Fotos: data/raw/photos/places_k45/hohlstrasse/NN.jpg
Hinweis: Tramlinien/Namen mit '?' unverifiziert; Hoehen aus OSM/Web; Farben aus Sicht (Naeherung).

## Charakter
Long mixed street: workshops, small shops, 1890-1930 apartment blocks, school Feldstrasse, Kanzlei; 358 trees, wide.

## Landmarks (Sichtbefunde)
Small stone wall fountain with female figure and round wall niche (photo 1), tub fountains with round granite bowl (photos 3,6?), WWF building (white, photo 4-5), orange-brown 6 storey 1930s block with flat roof (photo 2), 8-12 storey modern light-concrete + beige 1990s-2010s apartment blocks (photos 6-11: ceramic/brick pale beige facade, grid windows, ground floor commercial).

## Fassadenfarben/-stile
Pale beige/sand render, light-grey concrete grid, orange-brown plaster 1930s, blue shutters (photo 4).

## Bodenbelaege
Cobblestone (Kopfstein, WWF entrance), asphalt, concrete.

## Stadtmobiliar
Granite fountains, benches 79, trees 358, parking 89.

## OeV
Tram 2, 3, 8 at Hardplatz/Stauffacher; bus 31/72.

## Gruen
Many street trees.

## Laeden/Bars (Fakten)
Lucchinetti Motos, Edition Gut books, EquiTable, hairdressers, Schulzahnklinik.

## Atmosphaere
Quiet backstreet.
