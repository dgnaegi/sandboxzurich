# Hardbrücke + Bahnhof Hardbrücke (Kreis 5)
Lage: WGS84 47.3852,8.517; lokal x=26 y=122 (m, Radius der Analyse 200 m). Vorschau-Kontaktbogen: preview/ref_k45_hardbruecke.jpg, Fotos: data/raw/photos/places_k45/hardbruecke/NN.jpg
Hinweis: Tramlinien/Namen mit '?' unverifiziert; Hoehen aus OSM/Web; Farben aus Sicht (Naeherung).

## Charakter
Hardbruecke: elevated concrete motorway-like arterial bridge (1972, Hardbruecke / Pfingstweid) over the SBB tracks; Bahnhof Zuerich Hardbruecke (S-Bahn station, platforms under the bridge). Industrial-turned-trendy Zuerich-West entry. Loud, heavy traffic above, rail below.

## Landmarks (Sichtbefunde)
Bahnhof platforms (photos 3,4): long platform in concrete + black steel canopy; red-painted (RAL ~3020 red/orange-red) rear walls with posters, tactile guide strips (white), glass facade (translucent, Vitrine) with large perforated/louvre metal cube 'Platform' office building (photo 4). Station hall roof in black lattice steel (photo 1), sign 'Gleis 2+3', blue station sign board white text 'Zuerich Hardbruecke' on raw board-formed concrete (photo 9). Staircase/elevator glass shelters (photo 2) with a red bridge-deck building ('Mobility / red' tint) at the road bridge. Tracks: ~72 rail ways, 42 switches, 19 signals within r=200. Prime Tower (126 m) to N: teal glass, stepped staggered volumes (photos 6-8).

## Fassadenfarben/-stile
Platform building (OSM 'Platform', 25 m, #222222 dark, roof #999). Cubus (#ffffff, 25 m). Industrial brick Maag buildings behind. Concrete bridge deck: raw grey with form marks.

## Bodenbelaege
Asphalt on bridge, concrete platform, ballast (Schotter) on tracks, setts/paving stones at Geroldstrasse side.

## Stadtmobiliar
Elevators (9), bike parking, vending machines (8), waste baskets, railings in light-grey steel; DB-style departure displays; blue SBB signs.

## OeV
S-Bahn S3, S5, S6, S9, S12, S24 etc., tram 4, 13? (4 at Hardbruecke tram_stop), bus 72/32.

## Gruen
Sparse; 41 trees at foot of bridge.

## Laeden/Bars (Fakten)
Brezelkoenig, k kiosk, avec, Blueme Lade, Coop to go, Clouds bar (top of Prime Tower), Gerolds Garten and Gerolds Markt at Geroldstrasse, Helsinki nightclub, Zum Gaul, Rosso.

## Atmosphaere
Dusk (photo 5): glass facade glows amber, long diesel/electric double-deck trains; rail yard stretch.
