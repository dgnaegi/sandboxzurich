## Charakter
Europaallee: 2010s SBB development at HB West, rationalist stone-clad office/retail/school blocks (PH Zuerich Pestalozzianum), granite paving, UBS/Credit Suisse.

## Landmarks (Sichtbefunde)
Pale concrete/stone-clad (light beige-grey #d0c8bb) boxy blocks of 7-12 storeys with regular gridded windows (photos 7-10: some with pink/rose accents, some dark grey), wide pedestrian boulevard with continuous cobble strip and granite paving (photos 2,3), granite round bin (photo 6), young trees in square pits (photos 2,3,4,7), outdoor seating with red umbrellas (photo 4), building crane skyline (photo 5: yellow cranes, red S-Bahn trains). UBS big black portal (photo 6), Credit Suisse at corners (photos 1,11).

## Fassadenfarben/-stile
Beige-grey stone, white aluminium (photo 9: white office with light-blue glass), dark grey, pale pink accents.

## Bodenbelaege
Granite slabs (light grey, tooled), asphalt, cobble strip, concrete.

## Stadtmobiliar

## OeV

## Gruen

## Laeden/Bars (Fakten)
