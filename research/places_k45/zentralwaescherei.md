# Zentralwäscherei (Kreis 5)
Lage: WGS84 47.3871,8.5204; lokal x=280 y=337 (m, Radius der Analyse 120 m). Vorschau-Kontaktbogen: preview/ref_k45_zentralwaescherei.jpg, Fotos: data/raw/photos/places_k45/zentralwaescherei/NN.jpg
Hinweis: Tramlinien/Namen mit '?' unverifiziert; Hoehen aus OSM/Web; Farben aus Sicht (Naeherung).

## Charakter
Former central laundry (1920s-70s industrial); now community/culture/maker space (Zentralwaescherei Zuerich, Bitwaescherei, Kleinwaescherei restaurant). OSM height 26.2 m, 6 levels, flat roof.

## Landmarks (Sichtbefunde)
Block: large plaster wall painted pink/graffiti with poster pasteup; white-black lettering 'Zentral waescherei' on facade (photo 1). Roof-terrace balcony view of glass-curtain-wall offices (photo 2) with rough wooden rails. Interior: workshop (photo 3). Neon/yellow-green tile backdrop (photo 4).

## Fassadenfarben/-stile
Plaster pink (#d4909e) at street side, graffiti/posters; grey concrete upper floors.

## Bodenbelaege
Asphalt, gravel; tram track nearby (Neue Hard).

## Stadtmobiliar
Wild posters, wooden benches, chairs, container offices.

## OeV
Tram 4 Escher-Wyss/ Neue Hard bus; ~250 m to Hardbruecke.

## Gruen
Scarce, a few planted trees (13).

## Laeden/Bars (Fakten)
Kleinwaescherei, ReCreaZZZ, Kantine im 5i, Josy, RepairHub.

## Atmosphaere
Alternative/DIY, heavy graffiti, posters.
