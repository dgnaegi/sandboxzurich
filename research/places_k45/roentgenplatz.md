# Röntgenplatz (Kreis 5)
Lage: WGS84 47.3852,8.5268; lokal x=766 y=133 (m, Radius der Analyse 110 m). Vorschau-Kontaktbogen: preview/ref_k45_roentgenplatz.jpg, Fotos: data/raw/photos/places_k45/roentgenplatz/NN.jpg
Hinweis: Tramlinien/Namen mit '?' unverifiziert; Hoehen aus OSM/Web; Farben aus Sicht (Naeherung).

## Charakter
Quiet residential square (1920s Genossenschafts-Siedlung); triangular sand-gravel plaza with kerb frame, fountains, flags.

## Landmarks (Sichtbefunde)
Triangular plaza (photos 5,6): pale beige gravel (Mergel/Kies) fill within a raised light-concrete kerb (triangle), plane trees along edges. Granite triangular fountain basin with figure group (photo 3). Rough concrete block fountain (photo 8). Checkerboard roundabout marking (photo 7): black/white painted circle on asphalt. Flagpoles (3: white, red, blue; photo 1). Block corner building: red-pink plaster with arched gate (photos 2,10): 5 storeys, hipped dark-brown tile roof with dormers, window shutters red (RAL 3016-ish) in groups, round-arch entrance with iron gate, bollard balls in stone at gate. Historic school building (photo 11): rusticated stone ground floor with red arched windows, red-pink plaster upper floors, grey slate mansard roof.

## Fassadenfarben/-stile
Pink/salmon (#d99a8a), brick red (#b04a40) plaster, light grey rustica; red shutters; hipped roofs 1919-1927.

## Bodenbelaege
Gravel plaza, light asphalt, granite curbs, small-sett inlay.

## Stadtmobiliar
Lamps hanging on wires (round white), benches, pétanque-friendly gravel, SP5 banner (Roentgenplatzfest).

## OeV
Tram 4/13 Limmatplatz 250 m E; bus 31 Roentgenplatz.

## Gruen
Plane trees (large) in rows; hedges around fountain.

## Laeden/Bars (Fakten)
Local; see OSM.

## Atmosphaere
Calm, sunlit, village-like.
