## Charakter
Sihl river quarter in Kreis 4/Wiedikon edge: Sportanlage Sihlhoelzli (1900s), Musikpavillon (1937), Stauffacherquai, Schimmelstrasse/Schoentalstrasse.

## Landmarks (Sichtbefunde)
Musikpavillon Sihlhoelzli (photos 1-5): concrete half-shell dome (cantilevered shell, grey-beige concrete), inner wood panelling (dark red-brown boards, #8a3a30), steel mesh safety net, small steps, white pipes, lamps; sits among tall trees/lime avenues (photos 2,4). Old skating photos (6-10) show large open flat ground (pond) with conifers backgrounds in 1900. Sporthalle (OSM #fdb, 3 levels, hipped roof #aab).

## Fassadenfarben/-stile
