# Lochergut (Kreis 4)
Lage: WGS84 47.3757,8.5177; lokal x=93 y=-933 (m, Radius der Analyse 150 m). Vorschau-Kontaktbogen: preview/ref_k45_lochergut.jpg, Fotos: data/raw/photos/places_k45/lochergut/NN.jpg
Hinweis: Tramlinien/Namen mit '?' unverifiziert; Hoehen aus OSM/Web; Farben aus Sicht (Naeherung).

## Charakter
1963-66 high-rise residential complex (architects Ernst Gisel? not verified; Locher & Cie yard) of 6 stepped slab houses at Seebahnstrasse + one smaller block; Kreis 4 landmark, with shopping base (Coop, pharmacy, Bistro, Grand Cafe) and Stadt/Quartier. OSM: Seebahnstrasse 175/177/181 21 levels 53-61 m, tail block 12 levels 40 m.

## Landmarks (Sichtbefunde)
Stepped slab: yellow-beige (#c9b27a) vertical end walls (ceramic/ Eternit tile?) with grey-brown horizontal balcony bands and dark window strips (photos 3,4,5), stepping heights (tallest 21 storeys, others lower). Base: low white/grey concrete podium with canopy, 'Zentrum Lochergut' letters (photo 2), grey board-formed concrete, ground floor glazed shop front, 1960s steel lettering. Rear tracks of Seebahn railway cutting with graffiti retaining wall (photo 5), tram stop (photo 6). Ghost bike memorial (photo 7).

## Fassadenfarben/-stile
Yellow-beige tile ends, grey/brown balcony railings with sun-awnings, white/grey base.

## Bodenbelaege
Asphalt, concrete, tram tracks in asphalt, paving stones in shopping court.

## Stadtmobiliar
Round-lamp masts, shopfront signs, bike racks, street lamps (12).

## OeV
Tram 2/3 Lochergut (Badenerstrasse), tram 8/9? Sihlfeld; bus 31.

## Gruen
138 trees, planted greenery court.

## Laeden/Bars (Fakten)
Coop, Lochergut Apotheke, Grand Cafe Lochergut, Bistro Lochergut, Blumen Haab, Meyer's pub, Aquarium cafe, K-Street.

## Atmosphaere
1960s brutalist-friendly; busy shopping base.
