# Im Viadukt / Markthalle / Viaduktbögen (Kreis 5)
Lage: WGS84 47.3877,8.525; lokal x=626 y=409 (m, Radius der Analyse 220 m). Vorschau-Kontaktbogen: preview/ref_k45_viadukt.jpg, Fotos: data/raw/photos/places_k45/viadukt/NN.jpg
Hinweis: Tramlinien/Namen mit '?' unverifiziert; Hoehen aus OSM/Web; Farben aus Sicht (Naeherung).

## Charakter
Stone railway viaduct (1894, Lettenviadukt/Aussersihler Viadukt, SBB) running E-W along Viaduktstrasse; 36 arches converted 2004-10 into shops/cafes/studios; Markthalle Im Viadukt (2010) as glazed hall at the Limmatstrasse end. Hip, creative, foodie. Viaduct top = public promenade/gleis with Hardbruecke-side greenery.

## Landmarks (Sichtbefunde)
Arch arcade: rough-hewn grey-beige natural stone (Bruchstein/Quader) with round arches ~5-6 m wide, ~7-9 m high, steel-lattice girder deck on top with railing; arches infilled by black-framed glass shopfronts set back; numbered house numbers (e.g. 13, 14) painted on the arch spandrels (seen Josefwiese photo 2). Markthalle: OSM 1 level, 2010, glass/steel hall inserted between the arches (local ~718,410). Lettenviadukt over Limmat (photos 1,8): steel arch bridge with latticed parapet, painted grey-blue/green, Limmat turquoise-green, graffiti on quay wall. Old 1890s photos (3,6,7,9) show the stone arcade before conversion.

## Fassadenfarben/-stile
Arches: stone grey-beige (#a89f8f approx), warm; infill glass black frames; wooden/black signage. Houses opposite (Viaduktstrasse 23-71, 2010 new low commercial 2-storey boxes under the arcade side) light concrete/glass.

## Bodenbelaege
Cobblestone/Kopfstein strips in front of arches (Josefwiese photo 2, viadukt photo 2), asphalt road, gravel (fine_gravel/pebblestone) in outdoor cafe areas (photo 5).

## Stadtmobiliar
Brass/bronze bent-pipe drinking fountain (viadukt photo 2), planter beds, benches (42), bicycle racks (39), bollards (10), 27 surveillance cams. Parasol seating (red/orange/yellow umbrellas) under the steel girders at the Limmat quay cafe (photo 5).

## OeV
Tram 4/13 Limmatplatz, tram 17 Escher-Wyss-Platz; Bahnhof Hardbruecke 300 m W; the viaduct itself carries S-Bahn (rail:5).

## Gruen
Josefwiese N side, plane trees (271 trees within r=220), clipped hedges along arch frontage, gleis-side vegetation on viaduct top.

## Laeden/Bars (Fakten)
See OSM list: Markthalle (Restaurant Markthalle, Suedhang am Markt wine, Suedhang Mini-Metzg, Berg und Tal deli, Luchs & Hase), Don Weber bar, Zooloose/Fashionslave/Street-Files/Collectif College/Cabinet clothes, Qwstion bags, The Bagel Shop, Ambrosi cafe, Sibler houseware, St. Jakob Beck bakery, Westfluegel interior, Keen public bath. Jenseits Ruhepol/Kindertreff Viadukt community.

## Atmosphaere
Daytime: pedestrians, cargo bikes, tables outside the arches; evenings: warm light spilling from glass infill into grey stone; trains rumbling above.
