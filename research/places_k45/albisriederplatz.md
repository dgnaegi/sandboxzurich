# Albisriederplatz (Kreis 4)
Lage: WGS84 47.37815,8.5103; lokal x=-469 y=-668 (m, Radius der Analyse 140 m). Vorschau-Kontaktbogen: preview/ref_k45_albisriederplatz.jpg, Fotos: data/raw/photos/places_k45/albisriederplatz/NN.jpg
Hinweis: Tramlinien/Namen mit '?' unverifiziert; Hoehen aus OSM/Web; Farben aus Sicht (Naeherung).

## Charakter
Intersection Albisrieder-/Badenerstrasse/Hardau; tram terminus (2) loop, 1950s concrete tram shelters; surrounding Jugendstil corner houses.

## Landmarks (Sichtbefunde)
Flat concrete canopy shelter with curved edge and glass screens (photos 3,7): thin white concrete roof (1950s), glass windbreak, pole-mounted clock; Jugendstil corner house with turret and balconies, ochre/orange plaster with grey stone (photo 4,3); 1960s office/apartment building beige/grey (photo 2). Blue-white Tram 2000 on tracks (photo 9), trees lining Albisriederstrasse boulevard (10,11: plane trees, wide, tracks in centre).

## Fassadenfarben/-stile
Ochre/orange (#d9a35a) plaster, light stone, pink; mansard.

## Bodenbelaege
Asphalt, yellow zebra markings, tram tracks, stone kerbs.

## Stadtmobiliar
Tall poles with lamp heads, clocks, cabinets, VBZ ticket kiosk.

## OeV
Tram 2, 3, bus 31/72/83/89.

## Gruen
Plane trees (71).

## Laeden/Bars (Fakten)
Kuhn Back & Gastro, Hornecker, Naegeli Tabakfass, Interdiscount, Burger King.

## Atmosphaere
Central square of Aussersihl/ Albisrieden transit.
