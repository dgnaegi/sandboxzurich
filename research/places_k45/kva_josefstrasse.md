# Energiezentrale / KVA Josefstrasse (Kamin) (Kreis 5)
Lage: WGS84 47.38685,8.52224; lokal x=419 y=311 (m, Radius der Analyse 140 m). Vorschau-Kontaktbogen: preview/ref_k45_kva_josefstrasse.jpg, Fotos: data/raw/photos/places_k45/kva_josefstrasse/NN.jpg
Hinweis: Tramlinien/Namen mit '?' unverifiziert; Hoehen aus OSM/Web; Farben aus Sicht (Naeherung).

## Charakter
Energiezentrale Josefstrasse (replacement heating plant) at the old KVA Josefstrasse site; Kehrichtverbrennung operated until ~2020/21, chimney remains Zuerich-West landmark.

## Landmarks (Sichtbefunde)
OLD chimney (photos 2,3,6): slender cylindrical white concrete shaft ~87-92 m (sources differ), plain, flat top ring, white steam plume; also a banded variant in photos 1 and 5: white shaft with 3 red horizontal bands on the upper third with a crown ring (the new Energiezentrale chimney, photos 1,5, with blue-grey glass cubic hall). 1910 postcard (photo 4): first building as red brick/stone Tudor-ish villa with mansard roof + low hall + thin tall brick chimney. Stone Viaduct arches (photo 6) with rail on top in foreground. Energiezentrale: glazed blue-grey cube volumes, dark aluminium, white pipes. Graffiti removal 2021 ("KCBRYNOTMAFS"), peregrine falcon nest box at top.

## Fassadenfarben/-stile
Grey-blue glass cladding, white painted steel, concrete; old buildings stone/brick.

## Bodenbelaege
Gravel, asphalt; Josefstrasse/Viaduktstrasse.

## Stadtmobiliar
Fences, pipes, rails.

## OeV
Tram 4/13 Limmatplatz, 17.

## Gruen
Josefwiese across road.

## Laeden/Bars (Fakten)
Josy, RepairHub, The Chair, Codes, IDA 14 interiors.

## Atmosphaere
Always visible from Josefwiese and Hardbruecke; plume of steam.
