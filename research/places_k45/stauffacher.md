# Stauffacher / Kirche St. Jakob (Kreis 4)
Lage: WGS84 47.373,8.529; lokal x=951 y=-1221 (m, Radius der Analyse 160 m). Vorschau-Kontaktbogen: preview/ref_k45_stauffacher.jpg, Fotos: data/raw/photos/places_k45/stauffacher/NN.jpg
Hinweis: Tramlinien/Namen mit '?' unverifiziert; Hoehen aus OSM/Web; Farben aus Sicht (Naeherung).

## Charakter
Large tram junction on Badenerstrasse/Stauffacherstrasse with Neo-Gothic church St. Jakob (1899, regional heritage); Stauffacherquai at Sihl; cooperative 'Zentrum Stauffacher'.

## Landmarks (Sichtbefunde)
St. Jakob: tall slender spire (Turmspitze ~ 70 m) with clock, grey-blue copper roof (photo 4,7), stone body brown-beige rustic ashlar with buttresses (photos 1,2,10); church forecourt: grey gravel with Schreitende bronze figures (photos 2,5), floating concrete staircase and red glass wall (photos 1,3: modern red-painted steel/glass barrier + white concrete cantilever stairs). Street: broad tram tracks in asphalt, light-ball street lamps on concrete posts (photos 6,8,9): tall grey concrete column with 2-3 round white globes. Buildings: 5-6 storey 1890-1910 corner houses in stone/yellow-grey plaster, turret roofs, mansard (photo 6,4). Bollards: large rough cubic granite bollards (photos 6,7).

## Fassadenfarben/-stile
Limestone grey, sandstone beige, plastered cream; mansards dark grey slate; shopfronts at ground floor with awnings.

## Bodenbelaege
Asphalt, tram track bed, granite kerbs, sett/cobbles near Werdgasse, gravel at church forecourt.

## Stadtmobiliar
Globe lamps on concrete masts, VBZ shelters, benches, bike racks.

## OeV
Tram 2/3/8/9 (badener/ Stauffacher), bus 31.

## Gruen
154 trees: plane/ linden.

## Laeden/Bars (Fakten)
Masala, Optik am Stauffacher, Berg-Apotheke, Coop-to-go (photo 9), beauty salons, medix.

## Atmosphaere
Dense, tram bells, church bells.
