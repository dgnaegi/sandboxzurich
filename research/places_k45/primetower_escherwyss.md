# Prime Tower / Escher-Wyss-Platz / Maag-Areal (Kreis 5)
Lage: WGS84 47.389,8.5195; lokal x=209 y=547 (m, Radius der Analyse 260 m). Vorschau-Kontaktbogen: preview/ref_k45_primetower_escherwyss.jpg, Fotos: data/raw/photos/places_k45/primetower_escherwyss/NN.jpg
Hinweis: Tramlinien/Namen mit '?' unverifiziert; Hoehen aus OSM/Web; Farben aus Sicht (Naeherung).

## Charakter
Zuerich-West business/culture core: Prime Tower (2011, Gigon/Guyer, 126 m, 36 levels, tallest building in Switzerland at the time), Maag-Areal (Lichthalle, Maag Music Hall), Escher-Wyss-Platz (tram/bus hub, Hardbruecke/Pfingstweid junction), Viaduct arches near Geroldstrasse.

## Landmarks (Sichtbefunde)
Prime Tower: octagonal-ish, stepped/staggered massing (shifted storeys), green-teal reflective glass in dark thin mullion grid (OSM building:colour #7ac2b4), dark overhanging plinth with white italic 'PRIME TOWER' letters on a black soffit (photos 2-7), flat roof (concrete), top with small dark projection. Cubus and Platform sit as 25 m glass boxes SW. Stone viaduct arch (photos 8-10): rough natural stone, round arch ~6 m wide, view through to Prime Tower, bollards in a row, cobble/asphalt, bike lane border strip. Maag Lichthalle: brick industrial with sawtooth glazing (heritage).

## Fassadenfarben/-stile
Prime Tower teal; surrounding offices light stone/aluminium; heritage Escher-Wyss factory brick yellow-brown (1891-93).

## Bodenbelaege
Asphalt, concrete slabs, sett pavement, gravel in Turbinenplatz side.

## Stadtmobiliar
286 trees (planted rows), 86 benches, 56 bike racks, 41 waste baskets, recycling points.

## OeV
Tram 4 (Hardbruecke / Escher-Wyss-Platz), tram 17, bus 32/33/72/83, S-Bahn Hardbruecke.

## Gruen
Tree-lined boulevards; green strip along Pfingstweidstrasse.

## Laeden/Bars (Fakten)
Clouds (restaurant/bar at top of Prime Tower), Zuercher Kantonalbank, Marronistand, Frau Gerolds Garten, Moods, Maag Music Hall, Kulturpark.

## Atmosphaere
Night photo 1: tower lit by grid of office windows; yellow light streaks of traffic on Hardbruecke.
