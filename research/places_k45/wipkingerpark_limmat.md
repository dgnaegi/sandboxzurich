# Limmatufer / Wipkingerpark (Kreis 5)
Lage: WGS84 47.3925,8.5216; lokal x=362 y=939 (m, Radius der Analyse 220 m). Vorschau-Kontaktbogen: preview/ref_k45_wipkingerpark_limmat.jpg, Fotos: data/raw/photos/places_k45/wipkingerpark_limmat/NN.jpg
Hinweis: Tramlinien/Namen mit '?' unverifiziert; Hoehen aus OSM/Web; Farben aus Sicht (Naeherung).

## Charakter
Limmat river stretch N of Hardbruecke with Wipkingerpark (green strip along river) and Wipkingen hillside houses; Wipkingerviadukt; Hardturm area.

## Landmarks (Sichtbefunde)
Limmat: wide turquoise-green flowing river with concrete/stone embankment steps (photos 8,9), willows (Trauerweiden, photos 6,8,9,10), poplars (photo 4). Wipkinger Viadukt: steel arch rail bridge (viadukt photo 1) above river, grey-white paint, stone abutments. Modern white 5-storey office/school blocks on S bank (photo 9,10). Pavilion/low white building with sawtooth/folded roof (photo 1: Flussbad? white cube with zigzag concrete awning, green lawn). Allotment gardens with brown wooden shed on slope (photo 7). Prime Tower and Hochhaus (Migros?) visible downriver (viadukt photos 8,10,11: tall light-grey tower with red lettering).

## Fassadenfarben/-stile
Hillside: plastered rendered houses beige, pink (#e6a99a), yellow, white, hipped/gabled red-brown tile roofs; modern white blocks.

## Bodenbelaege
Grass banks, stepped concrete quay, gravel paths, cobble near the Viadukt arches.

## Stadtmobiliar
Benches 23, picnic tables 7, drinking fountains, Ampèresteg.

## OeV
Tram 4/13? Escher-Wyss-Platz; bus 72; S-Bahn Hardbruecke / Wipkingen.

## Gruen
Willows, grass.

## Laeden/Bars (Fakten)
Wipkingerplatz cafes; Kafi Tintefisch; Bike Kitchen.

## Atmosphaere
Open river, swimmers in summer, hillside backdrop.
