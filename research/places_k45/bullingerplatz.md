# Bullingerplatz (Kreis 4)
Lage: WGS84 47.37965,8.516; lokal x=-41 y=-496 (m, Radius der Analyse 100 m). Vorschau-Kontaktbogen: preview/ref_k45_bullingerplatz.jpg, Fotos: data/raw/photos/places_k45/bullingerplatz/NN.jpg
Hinweis: Tramlinien/Namen mit '?' unverifiziert; Hoehen aus OSM/Web; Farben aus Sicht (Naeherung).

## Charakter
Quiet Genossenschaftsplatz in Hard quarter with circular fountain and Bullingerkirche tower (1925/1945), trolleybus (Trolley 32?) turning loop? Rathaus Hard.

## Landmarks (Sichtbefunde)
Round granite basin fountain with wide, shallow shell (photos 1,4) and central jet; Bullingerkirche: slender white-beige clock tower (photos 1,2,8) with blue clock, flat top; low yellow-ochre (#d9c06a) 2-storey Rathaus Hard? with hipped tile roof, shutters green/beige (photo 3). Corner block: salmon/pink plaster (#e0a78a) 5 storey with hipped tile roof, green shutters (photo 4). Historic VBZ buses (Oldtimer) in blue (#2a6fb0) circling the roundabout (photos 6-11).

## Fassadenfarben/-stile
Ochre, salmon, cream; green/beige shutters; hipped red roofs 1928-1931.

## Bodenbelaege
Asphalt roundabout, granite kerbs, planter beds.

## Stadtmobiliar
Planters, cargo bike (photo 5), lamps.

## OeV
Bus 31 Bullingerplatz / trolley bus 32 loop (blue).

## Gruen
91 trees, planted islands.

## Laeden/Bars (Fakten)
Fontana Apotheke, Cafe du Bonheur, An Chay.

## Atmosphaere
Calm, trees.
