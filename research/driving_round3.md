# Driving round 3 (car crashes, knock-over objects)

Cause: all 4076 street lamps are cable lamps hanging 7.6 m above the carriageway (props.ts T.lamp), but tiles.ts registered a 0.15 m ground collider for each. Measured with tiles.onRoad: 149/150, 90/91, 100/102, 48/49 lamp circles lie on the road mask (Hardstrasse, Langstrasse, Seebahnstrasse, Badenerstrasse). Phantom poles in the lane = 12 of 15 forward crashes (3-4 hits each at 4-7 m/s, damage 0.02-0.08 per hit). Fix: lamps no longer get a collider (masts, real poles, stay hard).
Also found: the car's collision circles sit in two columns (lat +-0.6), so a thin post in the middle of the car slipped through the gap. New `Colliders.knockCapsule` covers the whole body.

Measurement: web/tests/drive_route.mjs (autopilot on the OSM road graph, 8 streets, ~2.7 km, real NPC traffic/parked cars). Forward crashes (speed > 0.5 m/s at impact): before 15 on 2.73 km (5.5/km), after 6 on 2.78 km (2.2/km). The rest: trees at the kerb within 1.5 m of the lane centre, parked/NPC cars the autopilot does not brake for. Reversing crashes are autopilot recovery noise (not counted).

Knock-over (world/knockable.ts): bins (kind 1, solid for walkers/bikes as before), bollards, bike racks, traffic sign posts with their plates (kind 2: new colliders seen only by the player's car, so walkers/bikes/NPC traffic are unchanged). Tile builders put vertex ranges in `mesh.userData.knock`; on contact the range is collapsed in the merged geometry and a small proxy mesh (copy of that range, child of the tile group) tips over with a 0.55 s animation driven by onBeforeRender. No crash, no damage, -1.5 % speed per object. Stays down until the tile rebuilds; proxy geometry is disposed with the tile; colliders removed with key `<tile>#knock`. Not instanced (the sources are merged meshes). Traffic signals, hydrants, masts, trees, benches stay hard. Test: web/tests/knock.mjs.
